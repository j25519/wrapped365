ABOUT THE NUMBERS

How Wrapped 365 counts a play

Wrapped 365 reads the music events from your Spotify Extended Streaming History archive. Only tracks played for at least 30 seconds contribute to the play counts and rankings. Listening duration is the actual ms_played reported by Spotify, not the full song length.

Tracks with the same artist and title are grouped across releases. Album statistics use the album name and artist. Podcasts, video plays, and records missing music metadata are excluded. Explicit skips and shuffle/offline statistics include valid music events even if they lasted under 30 seconds.

All dates are grouped by UTC because Spotify records playback end times in UTC. Wrapped 365 discards usernames, IP addresses, user agents, and other unrelated information without saving them.

These figures aren't guaranteed to match Spotify Wrapped, which applies its own eligibility rules and reporting windows.
