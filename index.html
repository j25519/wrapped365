      ABOUT THE NUMBERS

      How Wrapped 365 counts a play

      Wrapped 365 reads the music events from your Spotify Extended Streaming History archive. Only tracks played for at least 30 seconds contribute to the play counts and rankings. Listening duration is the actual ms_played reported by Spotify, not the full song length.

      Tracks with the same primary artist and title are grouped across releases. When a source provides multiple credited artists, each receives artist level play credit and all names appear in track listings. The standard Spotify Extended Streaming History frequently provides only one album artist, so the app cannot reliably reconstruct missing credits. Album statistics use the album name and primary artist.

      Podcasts, video plays, and records missing music metadata are excluded. Explicit skips and shuffle/offline statistics include valid music events even if they lasted under 30 seconds.

      When Spotify supplies only one artist, Wrapped 365 can infer featured artists from explicit credits in track titles or artist names (such as (feat. Artist)); inferred credits are best effort, not verified metadata.

      All dates are grouped by UTC because Spotify records playback end times in UTC. Wrapped 365 discards usernames, IP addresses, user agents, and other unrelated information without saving them.

      These figures aren't guaranteed to match Spotify Wrapped, which applies its own eligibility rules and reporting windows.
